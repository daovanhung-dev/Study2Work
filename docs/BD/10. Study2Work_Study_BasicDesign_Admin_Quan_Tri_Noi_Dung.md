# Study2Work - Study
## Basic Design: Admin Quan Tri Noi Dung

> **STT:** 10  
> **Nguon:** `0. Study2Work_Study_Business_Description.md`  
> **Pham vi:** Mo ta chuc nang Admin quan tri lo trinh, khoa hoc, chuong, bai hoc, tai lieu va bai tap. Khong mo ta API, database, source code, storage hoac pipeline xuat ban ky thuat.

---

## 1. Muc tieu module

Module Admin Quan tri Noi dung giup doi ngu Study van hanh noi dung hoc tap ma khong can developer can thiep cho cac thay doi thong thuong.

Muc tieu:

- Tao, sua, sap xep, xuat ban va luu tru noi dung hoc.
- Quan ly cau truc **Lo trinh -> Khoa hoc -> Chuong -> Bai hoc/Bai tap**.
- Cau hinh noi dung bat buoc/tuy chon, bai hoc mau, dieu kien mo khoa va hoan thanh.
- Kiem soat vong doi noi dung tu ban nhap den xuat ban.
- Dam bao noi dung co nguon, quyen su dung va nguoi chiu trach nhiem.

---

## 2. Doi tuong su dung

| Vai tro | Chuc nang lien quan |
|---|---|
| Content Admin | Bien soan, sua, sap xep va gui noi dung duyet. |
| Admin | Xuat ban, luu tru, cau hinh dieu kien va xem anh huong. |
| Super Admin | Quan ly quyen, phe duyet thao tac quan trong neu can. |
| Community Moderator | Chi quan ly phan cong dong neu duoc cap quyen, khong sua noi dung hoc. |

---

## 3. Vong doi noi dung

```text
DRAFT
-> IN_REVIEW
-> PUBLISHED
-> UPDATED
-> ARCHIVED
```

| Trang thai | Y nghia |
|---|---|
| `DRAFT` | Noi dung dang bien soan, khong hien thi cho hoc vien. |
| `IN_REVIEW` | Noi dung cho kiem tra chuyen mon/bien tap. |
| `PUBLISHED` | Noi dung dang duoc cung cap cho hoc vien. |
| `UPDATED` | Co phien ban/chinh sua quan trong sau xuat ban. |
| `ARCHIVED` | Khong mo cho hoc vien moi, nhung du lieu lich su duoc giu. |

## 4. Chuc nang chi tiet

### 4.1. Quan ly lo trinh

Admin co the:

- Tao lo trinh moi.
- Cap nhat ten, slug, mo ta, anh/bieu tuong.
- Cau hinh doi tuong phu hop/khong phu hop.
- Cau hinh kien thuc dau vao va ky nang dau ra.
- Gan danh sach khoa hoc theo thu tu.
- Danh dau khoa hoc bat buoc/tuy chon.
- Cau hinh dieu kien hoan thanh lo trinh.
- Cau hinh lo trinh goi y tiep theo.
- Gan nhom Zalo lien quan.
- Xuat ban, tam an hoac luu tru lo trinh.

### 4.2. Quan ly khoa hoc

Admin co the:

- Tao khoa hoc moi.
- Cap nhat ten, mo ta, cap do, thoi luong uoc tinh.
- Cau hinh muc tieu hoc tap.
- Cau hinh kien thuc yeu cau truoc khi hoc.
- Quan ly danh sach chuong.
- Gan khoa hoc vao mot hoac nhieu lo trinh.
- Cau hinh bai hoc mau.
- Cau hinh dieu kien hoan thanh khoa hoc.
- Gan nhom Zalo lien quan.
- Xuat ban, cap nhat hoac luu tru khoa hoc.

### 4.3. Quan ly chuong

Admin co the:

- Tao/sua/xoa chuong trong khoa hoc khi noi dung chua xuat ban hoac theo quy trinh cap nhat.
- Sap xep thu tu chuong.
- Cau hinh muc tieu chuong.
- Gan bai hoc va bai tap vao chuong.
- Cau hinh dieu kien mo khoa chuong.
- Cau hinh dieu kien hoan thanh chuong.

### 4.4. Quan ly bai hoc

Admin co the:

- Tao bai hoc moi.
- Cap nhat tieu de, mo ta, muc tieu.
- Gan video, bai doc, ghi chu, vi du, ma nguon mau.
- Gan tai lieu di kem.
- Cau hinh bai hoc bat buoc/tuy chon.
- Cau hinh dieu kien hoan thanh.
- Cau hinh bai hoc mau cong khai.
- Sap xep bai hoc trong chuong.
- An/xuat ban/cap nhat bai hoc.

### 4.5. Quan ly tai lieu va tai nguyen

Admin co the:

- Them tai lieu PDF/slide/markdown/link tham khao/code mau.
- Gan tai lieu voi bai hoc/khoa hoc.
- Danh dau tai lieu bat buoc hoac tham khao.
- Cap nhat mo ta tai lieu.
- Ghi nguon, quyen su dung va nguoi chiu trach nhiem.
- An tai lieu neu khong con hop le.

### 4.6. Quan ly bai tap

Admin co the:

- Tao bai tap trac nghiem, thuc hanh, tu luan, nop file/link.
- Gan bai tap voi bai hoc, chuong hoac khoa hoc.
- Cau hinh bai tap bat buoc/tuy chon.
- Cau hinh han nop neu co.
- Cau hinh dap an cho trac nghiem.
- Cau hinh rubric cho danh gia thu cong.
- Cau hinh cho phep nop lai.
- Cau hinh goi y/hint.

### 4.7. Sap xep noi dung

Admin can co cach sap xep:

- Thu tu khoa hoc trong lo trinh.
- Thu tu chuong trong khoa hoc.
- Thu tu bai hoc/bai tap trong chuong.

He thong can canh bao neu viec sap xep lam anh huong hoc vien dang hoc, vi du bai hoc da hoan thanh bi dua ve sau mot noi dung moi bat buoc.

### 4.8. Kiem tra truoc xuat ban

Truoc khi xuat ban, he thong can ho tro checklist:

- Co ten va mo ta.
- Co muc tieu hoc tap.
- Co cau truc noi dung hop le.
- Co dieu kien hoan thanh neu la noi dung bat buoc.
- Link/video/tai lieu khong rong.
- Noi dung co nguon va quyen su dung.
- Khong co bai hoc bat buoc bi thieu trong luong hoc.
- Neu co bai hoc mau, da cau hinh pham vi cong khai ro rang.

### 4.9. Cap nhat noi dung da xuat ban

Khi cap nhat noi dung da xuat ban:

- Admin can biet noi dung dang co bao nhieu hoc vien bi anh huong.
- He thong can phan biet sua nho va thay doi quan trong.
- Thay doi quan trong co the can thong bao hoc vien.
- Neu thay doi dieu kien hoan thanh, he thong can canh bao tac dong den tien do.
- Neu thay doi lon cau truc khoa hoc, nen tao phien ban moi hoac co thong bao ro.

## 5. Quy tac nghiep vu

| Ma | Quy tac |
|---|---|
| ADM-CONT-01 | Noi dung chua `PUBLISHED` khong hien thi cho hoc vien. |
| ADM-CONT-02 | Admin khong duoc xuat ban noi dung khong ro nguon/quyen su dung. |
| ADM-CONT-03 | Thay doi noi dung da xuat ban phai canh bao neu anh huong hoc vien dang hoc. |
| ADM-CONT-04 | Noi dung bat buoc phai co dieu kien hoan thanh ro rang. |
| ADM-CONT-05 | Bai hoc mau phai duoc cau hinh rieng, khong mac dinh cong khai. |
| ADM-CONT-06 | Khoa hoc co the nam trong nhieu lo trinh, nhung thay doi can xem tac dong tren tat ca lo trinh lien quan. |
| ADM-CONT-07 | Noi dung `ARCHIVED` khong mo cho hoc vien moi, nhung lich su hoc can duoc bao toan. |
| ADM-CONT-08 | Cac thao tac xuat ban, luu tru, thay doi dieu kien hoan thanh can co audit log. |

---

## 6. Tieu chi hoan tat module

- Admin co the quan ly lo trinh, khoa hoc, chuong, bai hoc, tai lieu va bai tap.
- Admin co the sap xep noi dung theo cau truc hoc.
- Admin co the cau hinh bat buoc/tuy chon, mo khoa, hoan thanh va bai hoc mau.
- Noi dung phai di qua trang thai vong doi truoc khi hien thi cho hoc vien.
- He thong canh bao tac dong khi cap nhat noi dung da xuat ban.
- Thao tac quan trong co lich su de truy vet.
