# Study2Work - Study
## Basic Design: Bai Tap va Danh Gia

> **STT:** 06  
> **Nguon:** `0. Study2Work_Study_Business_Description.md`  
> **Pham vi:** Mo ta chuc nang bai tap, nop bai, cham diem va phan hoi o muc nghiep vu. Khong mo ta sandbox code, API, database hoac co che xu ly file ky thuat.

---

## 1. Muc tieu module

Module Bai tap va Danh gia giup hoc vien chuyen tu "xem noi dung" sang "luyen tap va chung minh hieu bai".

Muc tieu:

- Cho phep hoc vien lam bai tap theo bai hoc, chuong hoac khoa hoc.
- Ho tro cac hinh thuc bai tap phu hop V1.
- Ghi nhan bai nop, trang thai danh gia va ket qua.
- Cho phep sua/nop lai khi bai can chinh sua.
- Cung cap thong tin danh gia de tinh tien do hoc tap.

---

## 2. Doi tuong su dung

| Vai tro | Chuc nang lien quan |
|---|---|
| Learner | Xem de bai, luu nhap, nop bai, xem ket qua va nop lai. |
| Admin/Mentor | Xem bai nop, danh gia thu cong, gui nhan xet, yeu cau sua. |
| Content Admin | Tao cau hinh bai tap qua module quan tri noi dung. |

---

## 3. Loai bai tap ho tro

| Loai | Mo ta | Khuyen nghi V1 |
|---|---|---|
| Trac nghiem | Kiem tra khai niem, cu phap, doc hieu | Bat buoc trong V1. |
| Bai thuc hanh co huong dan | Hoc vien lam theo tung buoc va doi chieu ket qua | Bat buoc trong V1. |
| Tu luan ngan | Giai thich y tuong, mo ta thuat toan | Nen co trong V1. |
| Nop file | Nop ma nguon, anh chup, tai lieu | Co the ho tro khi storage san sang. |
| Nop lien ket | Nop GitHub/GitLab/Google Drive/demo URL | Nen co trong V1 vi nhe va thuc te. |
| Cham code tu dong | Chay test case tren sandbox | De V2/V3. |

---

## 4. Cau truc bai tap

Mot bai tap can co:

- Ten bai tap.
- Muc tieu hoc tap.
- Mo ta yeu cau bang tieng Viet de hieu.
- Noi dung dau vao/dau ra neu la bai code.
- Dieu kien hoan thanh.
- Diem toi da hoac ket qua dat/khong dat neu co.
- Goi y theo tung muc.
- Tai nguyen tham khao.
- Han nop neu Admin cau hinh.
- Hinh thuc nop bai.
- Rubric danh gia neu can cham thu cong.
- Trang thai bat buoc/tuy chon doi voi tien do.

---

## 5. Chuc nang chi tiet

### 5.1. Xem danh sach bai tap

Hoc vien co the xem bai tap trong:

- Man hoc bai.
- Trang chi tiet chuong.
- Trang chi tiet khoa hoc.
- Dashboard neu bai tap dang can lam/can sua/sap den han.

Thong tin tren danh sach:

- Ten bai tap.
- Gan voi bai hoc/chuong/khoa hoc nao.
- Bat buoc hay tuy chon.
- Han nop neu co.
- Trang thai bai nop cua hoc vien.
- Ket qua danh gia moi nhat.

### 5.2. Xem de bai

Trang chi tiet bai tap can hien thi:

- Muc tieu bai tap.
- Noi dung yeu cau.
- Du lieu mau hoac ket qua ky vong.
- Tieu chi hoan thanh.
- Cac muc goi y.
- Tai lieu lien quan.
- Quy dinh nop bai.
- Trang thai bai nop hien tai.

Neu bai tap chua duoc mo khoa, he thong khong hien thi noi dung rieng tu va can neu ly do.

### 5.3. Luu nhap bai lam

Hoc vien co the luu nhap doi voi:

- Bai tu luan.
- Bai nop link.
- Bai nop file neu co.
- Bai thuc hanh co ghi chu tien trinh.

Quy tac:

- Ban nhap chua duoc xem la da nop.
- Ban nhap khong tinh hoan thanh.
- Hoc vien co the tiep tuc sua truoc khi nop.

### 5.4. Nop bai

Hinh thuc nop bai:

- Chon dap an trac nghiem.
- Nhap noi dung text.
- Tai file.
- Dan link repo/demo/tai lieu.
- Xac nhan da hoan thanh bai thuc hanh co huong dan.

Khi nop bai:

- He thong kiem tra cac truong bat buoc.
- He thong ghi nhan thoi diem nop.
- Trang thai chuyen `SUBMITTED`.
- Neu bai trac nghiem co dap an, he thong co the cham tu dong.
- Neu can danh gia thu cong, trang thai chuyen `UNDER_REVIEW`.

### 5.5. Cham bai va phan hoi

V1 ho tro:

- Cham tu dong cho trac nghiem.
- Kiem tra yeu cau nop voi file/lien ket.
- Admin/Mentor danh gia thu cong theo rubric.
- Gui nhan xet cho hoc vien.
- Danh dau ket qua: dat, can sua, chua dat.

Thong tin phan hoi nen gom:

- Ket qua.
- Diem neu co.
- Nhan xet ngan gon.
- Cac loi can sua.
- Han nop lai neu co.
- Tai lieu goi y de hoc lai.

### 5.6. Nop lai bai

Neu ket qua la `NEEDS_REVISION` hoac Admin cho phep, hoc vien co the nop lai.

Quy tac:

- Luu lich su cac lan nop o muc hien thi can thiet.
- Lan nop moi nhat la co so tinh trang thai hien tai.
- Neu het han nop lai, he thong can hien thi ro han che.
- Admin/Mentor co the mo lai quyen nop trong truong hop ngoai le.

---

## 6. Trang thai bai tap

```text
NOT_STARTED
-> DRAFT
-> SUBMITTED
-> UNDER_REVIEW
-> PASSED / NEEDS_REVISION / FAILED
```

| Trang thai | Y nghia |
|---|---|
| `NOT_STARTED` | Hoc vien chua bat dau lam. |
| `DRAFT` | Da co ban nhap nhung chua nop. |
| `SUBMITTED` | Da nop va dang cho xu ly. |
| `UNDER_REVIEW` | Dang duoc Admin/Mentor danh gia. |
| `PASSED` | Dat dieu kien bai tap. |
| `NEEDS_REVISION` | Can sua va nop lai. |
| `FAILED` | Khong dat theo quy dinh hien tai. |

## 7. Luong nghiep vu chinh

### 7.1. Lam va nop bai tap

```text
1. Hoc vien mo bai hoc hoac danh sach bai tap.
2. He thong kiem tra bai tap da duoc mo khoa.
3. Hoc vien xem de bai, tieu chi va goi y.
4. Hoc vien lam bai va co the luu nhap.
5. Hoc vien nop bai.
6. He thong ghi nhan bai nop va trang thai.
7. He thong cham tu dong hoac chuyen sang cho danh gia thu cong.
8. Hoc vien nhan ket qua va phan hoi.
9. Neu can sua, hoc vien nop lai theo quy dinh.
```

---

## 8. Quy tac nghiep vu

| Ma | Quy tac |
|---|---|
| EX-01 | Hoc vien chi lam/nop bai tap khi da co quyen truy cap noi dung lien quan. |
| EX-02 | Bai tap bat buoc anh huong den tien do; bai tap tuy chon khong chan hoan thanh neu Admin khong cau hinh. |
| EX-03 | Ban nhap khong duoc tinh la da nop. |
| EX-04 | Bai tap trac nghiem co the cham tu dong trong V1. |
| EX-05 | Bai code cham tu dong quy mo lon nam ngoai pham vi V1. |
| EX-06 | Ket qua `PASSED` moi duoc tinh la dat doi voi bai tap bat buoc. |
| EX-07 | Nop lai bai chi duoc thuc hien khi bai tap cho phep hoac Admin/Mentor mo lai. |
| EX-08 | Phan hoi can ro rang de hoc vien biet can sua gi, khong chi hien thi dat/truot. |

---

## 9. Tieu chi hoan tat module

- Hoc vien xem duoc bai tap theo bai hoc/chuong/khoa hoc.
- Hoc vien co the luu nhap, nop bai va xem trang thai bai nop.
- Trac nghiem co the tra ket qua theo cau hinh dap an.
- Bai can cham thu cong co trang thai cho danh gia va phan hoi.
- Bai tap bat buoc dong gop dung vao dieu kien tien do.
- Hoc vien co the nop lai khi bai can sua va duoc phep nop lai.
