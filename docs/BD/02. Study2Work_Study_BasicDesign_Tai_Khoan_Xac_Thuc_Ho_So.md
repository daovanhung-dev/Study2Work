# Study2Work - Study
## Basic Design: Tai Khoan, Xac Thuc va Ho So Hoc Vien

> **STT:** 02  
> **Nguon:** `0. Study2Work_Study_Business_Description.md`  
> **Pham vi:** Mo ta chuc nang tai khoan, dang nhap, xac thuc co ban va ho so hoc vien. Khong mo ta API, database, ma nguon hoac co che bao mat ky thuat chi tiet.

---

## 1. Muc tieu module

Module nay tao danh tinh hoc tap on dinh cho hoc vien trong Study. Danh tinh nay duoc dung de xac thuc nguoi dung, ca nhan hoa hanh trinh hoc, luu tien do, lien he khi can va ho tro cac truong hop ngoai le.

Muc tieu:

- Cho phep nguoi dung tao tai khoan va dang nhap an toan.
- Xac nhan kenh lien he co ban bang email hoac so dien thoai.
- Quan ly trang thai tai khoan theo hanh trinh hoc.
- Cho hoc vien cap nhat thong tin ho so trong pham vi cho phep.
- Dam bao Admin khong can thiep vao du lieu nhay cam nhu mat khau/OTP.

---

## 2. Doi tuong su dung

| Vai tro | Chuc nang lien quan |
|---|---|
| Guest | Dang ky, dang nhap, khoi phuc mat khau. |
| Learner | Dang xuat, doi mat khau, xac thuc kenh lien he, cap nhat ho so. |
| Learner Support | Xem thong tin ho tro tai khoan, khoa/mo tai khoan theo quy trinh. |
| Admin/Super Admin | Quan ly trang thai tai khoan va cac truong hop vi pham. |

---

## 3. Chuc nang chi tiet

### 3.1. Dang ky tai khoan

Nguoi dung co the dang ky tai khoan bang phuong thuc duoc he thong ho tro trong V1:

- Email va mat khau.
- So dien thoai va OTP neu ha tang san sang.
- Dang nhap ben thu ba la tuy chon cho giai doan sau.

Thong tin toi thieu khi dang ky:

| Truong | Bat buoc | Muc dich |
|---|---:|---|
| Ho va ten hien thi | Co | Ca nhan hoa giao dien va ho tro cong dong. |
| Email hoac so dien thoai | Co | Dang nhap, xac thuc va khoi phuc tai khoan. |
| Mat khau | Co neu dang ky noi bo | Xac thuc dang nhap. |
| Dong y dieu khoan | Co | Xac nhan chap nhan quy dinh Study. |

Sau khi dang ky thanh cong, tai khoan o trang thai `REGISTERED_PENDING_VERIFICATION`.

### 3.2. Xac thuc tai khoan

Xac thuc trong V1 chi nham xac nhan kenh lien he, khong phai KYC hoac xac minh phap ly.

Chuc nang:

- Gui link xac thuc email hoac ma OTP.
- Cho phep gui lai link/OTP trong gioi han chong spam.
- Hien thi trang thai da/chua xac thuc.
- Yeu cau xac thuc lai neu hoc vien thay doi email/so dien thoai.
- Chuyen tai khoan sang `VERIFIED` khi xac thuc thanh cong.

Quy tac:

- Hoc vien chua xac thuc khong the kich hoat lo trinh.
- Hoc vien chua xac thuc khong duoc luu tien do hoc tap chinh thuc.
- Admin khong nhin thay mat khau, OTP hoac link xac thuc rieng tu.
- Neu xac thuc that bai nhieu lan, he thong can tam thoi han che gui lai de tranh lam dung.

### 3.3. Dang nhap va dang xuat

Chuc nang dang nhap can:

- Cho phep dang nhap bang email/so dien thoai va mat khau neu dang ky noi bo.
- Thong bao loi ro rang khi sai thong tin dang nhap.
- Dieu huong nguoi dung sau dang nhap theo trang thai:
  - Chua xac thuc: toi man xac thuc.
  - Da xac thuc, chua onboarding: toi onboarding.
  - Da co lo trinh active: toi dashboard hoc vien.
  - Chua co lo trinh active: toi chon/kich hoat lo trinh.
- Cho phep dang xuat khoi phien hien tai.

### 3.4. Quen va doi mat khau

Chuc nang quen mat khau:

- Hoc vien nhap email/so dien thoai da dang ky.
- He thong gui lien ket hoac ma xac nhan dat lai mat khau.
- Hoc vien dat mat khau moi.
- He thong thong bao ket qua va yeu cau dang nhap lai neu can.

Chuc nang doi mat khau:

- Hoc vien da dang nhap nhap mat khau hien tai.
- Nhap mat khau moi va xac nhan.
- He thong thong bao thanh cong/thay bai.
- Neu phat hien rui ro, he thong co the yeu cau dang nhap lai.

### 3.5. Ho so hoc vien

Ho so hoc vien gom nhung nhom thong tin nghiep vu:

| Nhom du lieu | Vi du | Muc do chinh sua |
|---|---|---|
| Tai khoan | Email, so dien thoai, phuong thuc dang nhap | Can xac thuc lai khi thay doi. |
| Ho so co ban | Ten hien thi, avatar, tinh/thanh | Hoc vien tu sua. |
| Ho so hoc tap | Muc do nen tang, muc tieu, thoi gian hoc | Hoc vien tu sua; dong bo voi onboarding khi phu hop. |
| Du lieu tien do | Bai da hoc, diem, thoi diem hoan thanh | He thong tu ghi; hoc vien khong sua truc tiep. |
| Lich su ho tro | Yeu cau doi lo trinh, reset, khoa tai khoan | Xem theo quyen. |

Chuc nang cap nhat ho so:

- Cap nhat ten hien thi va avatar.
- Cap nhat tinh/thanh, truong/don vi, nganh nghe hien tai neu co.
- Cap nhat muc tieu hoc hoac thoi gian hoc du kien.
- Cap nhat cai dat thong bao ca nhan.
- Xem lich su lo trinh da tham gia o muc tong quan.

### 3.6. Quan ly trang thai tai khoan

Trang thai tai khoan:

```text
REGISTERED_PENDING_VERIFICATION
-> VERIFIED
-> ONBOARDING_IN_PROGRESS
-> READY_TO_LEARN
-> ACTIVE
-> SUSPENDED
```

| Trang thai | Y nghia |
|---|---|
| `REGISTERED_PENDING_VERIFICATION` | Da tao tai khoan nhung chua xac thuc kenh lien he. |
| `VERIFIED` | Da xac thuc kenh lien he nhung chua hoan tat onboarding. |
| `ONBOARDING_IN_PROGRESS` | Dang hoan thien onboarding. |
| `READY_TO_LEARN` | Da onboarding, chua kich hoat lo trinh. |
| `ACTIVE` | Co the hoc theo quyen hien co. |
| `SUSPENDED` | Bi tam ngung truy cap boi Admin. |

## 4. Luong nghiep vu chinh

### 4.1. Dang ky den xac thuc

```text
1. Guest chon Dang ky.
2. He thong thu thap thong tin tai khoan toi thieu.
3. Nguoi dung dong y dieu khoan.
4. He thong tao tai khoan chua xac thuc.
5. He thong gui email/link hoac OTP xac thuc.
6. Nguoi dung xac nhan kenh lien he.
7. He thong chuyen tai khoan sang VERIFIED.
8. He thong dieu huong sang onboarding.
```

### 4.2. Dang nhap theo trang thai

```text
1. Nguoi dung nhap thong tin dang nhap.
2. He thong kiem tra trang thai tai khoan.
3. Neu bi SUSPENDED, he thong thong bao han che truy cap.
4. Neu chua xac thuc, he thong dieu huong xac thuc.
5. Neu chua onboarding, he thong dieu huong onboarding.
6. Neu da san sang hoc, he thong dieu huong dashboard hoac trang kich hoat lo trinh.
```

## 5. Quy tac nghiep vu

| Ma | Quy tac |
|---|---|
| ACC-01 | Moi hoc vien can co mot danh tinh tai khoan duy nhat de luu tien do. |
| ACC-02 | Tai khoan chua xac thuc khong the kich hoat lo trinh hoac hoc noi dung rieng tu. |
| ACC-03 | Thay doi email/so dien thoai can xac thuc lai kenh lien he moi. |
| ACC-04 | Mat khau, ma OTP va lien ket xac thuc khong duoc hien thi cho Admin. |
| ACC-05 | Hoc vien khong duoc sua truc tiep du lieu tien do, diem hoac trang thai hoan thanh. |
| ACC-06 | Tai khoan `SUSPENDED` khong duoc tiep tuc hoc, nop bai hoac mo link cong dong rieng tu. |
| ACC-07 | Ho so chi thu thap du lieu can thiet cho hoc tap va ho tro. |

---

## 6. Tieu chi hoan tat module

- Guest co the dang ky tai khoan va nhan yeu cau xac thuc.
- Hoc vien co the dang nhap, dang xuat, quen mat khau va doi mat khau.
- He thong dieu huong dung theo trang thai tai khoan sau dang nhap.
- Hoc vien co the xem/cap nhat ho so trong pham vi cho phep.
- Tai khoan chua xac thuc, chua onboarding hoac bi tam ngung bi chan dung quy tac.
- Admin khong the xem du lieu xac thuc nhay cam.
