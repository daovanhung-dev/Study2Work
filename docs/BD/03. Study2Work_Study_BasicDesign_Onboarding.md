# Study2Work - Study
## Basic Design: Onboarding Hoc Vien

> **STT:** 03  
> **Nguon:** `0. Study2Work_Study_Business_Description.md`  
> **Pham vi:** Mo ta chuc nang onboarding bat buoc sau dang ky. Khong mo ta API, database hoac thuat toan goi y ky thuat.

---

## 1. Muc tieu module

Onboarding bien viec "tao tai khoan" thanh "bat dau mot hanh trinh hoc co chu dich". Module nay giup Study hieu muc tieu, nen tang va kha nang hoc cua hoc vien de goi y lo trinh phu hop.

Muc tieu:

- Thu thap thong tin hoc tap toi thieu, khong qua nhay cam.
- Giai thich quy tac hoc theo mot lo trinh dang hoat dong.
- Goi y lo trinh dua tren muc tieu va nen tang.
- Yeu cau hoc vien xac nhan thong tin truoc khi kich hoat lo trinh.
- Tao trang thai san sang hoc `READY_TO_LEARN`.

---

## 2. Doi tuong su dung

| Vai tro | Chuc nang lien quan |
|---|---|
| Learner | Hoan thanh onboarding, luu nhap, xem goi y lo trinh, xac nhan thong tin. |
| Learner Support | Xem trang thai onboarding de ho tro truong hop bat thuong. |
| Admin | Cau hinh cau hoi, danh sach muc tieu, danh sach cong nghe va lo trinh goi y neu can. |

---

## 3. Dieu kien bat dau va ket thuc

### 3.1. Dieu kien bat dau

Hoc vien duoc vao onboarding khi:

- Da tao tai khoan.
- Da xac thuc email hoac so dien thoai theo cau hinh V1.
- Tai khoan khong bi tam ngung.

### 3.2. Ket qua sau onboarding

Sau khi onboarding thanh cong, he thong can co:

- Ho so hoc tap co ban.
- Muc tieu hoc chinh cua hoc vien.
- Muc do nen tang lap trinh hien tai.
- Thoi gian hoc du kien moi tuan neu hoc vien cung cap.
- Danh sach lo trinh goi y.
- Mot lo trinh duoc hoc vien chon de xem xet/kich hoat.
- Ban xac nhan onboarding co thoi diem xac nhan.
- Trang thai tai khoan `READY_TO_LEARN` hoac tiep tuc kich hoat lo trinh neu flow gom buoc kich hoat.

---

## 4. Cac buoc onboarding

| Buoc | Noi dung | Bat buoc | Mo ta chuc nang |
|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Chao mung | Co | Giai thich Study, cach hoc theo lo trinh va quy tac mot lo trinh active. |
| 2 | Thong tin co ban | Mot phan | Thu thap ten hien thi, tinh/thanh, truong/don vi hoac tinh trang hien tai neu can. |
| 3 | Nen tang hien tai | Co | Hoc vien chon muc do lap trinh va cong nghe da biet. |
| 4 | Muc tieu hoc | Co | Hoc de qua mon, lam du an, thuc tap, chuyen nganh, tim viec hoac cung co nen tang. |
| 5 | Thoi gian hoc | Khuyen nghi | Thu thap so gio co the hoc moi tuan va khung gio ua thich. |
| 6 | Goi y lo trinh | Co | Hien thi cac lo trinh phu hop voi ho so vua nhap. |
| 7 | Chon lo trinh | Co | Hoc vien chon mot lo trinh duy nhat de tiep tuc. |
| 8 | Xac nhan | Co | Hoc vien kiem tra thong tin, dong y quy tac va hoan tat onboarding. |

---

## 5. Chuc nang chi tiet

### 5.1. Hien thi tien trinh onboarding

Onboarding can chia thanh cac buoc ngan, de hieu.

Chuc nang:

- Hien thi buoc hien tai va tong so buoc.
- Cho phep quay lai buoc truoc de sua thong tin.
- Canh bao neu nguoi dung roi trang khi co thay doi chua luu.
- Khong bat hoc vien nhap lai du lieu da luu hop le.

### 5.2. Luu nhap onboarding

Hoc vien co the dang lam onboarding va thoat giua chung.

Quy tac:

- He thong luu nhap cac cau tra loi da hop le.
- Khi dang nhap lai, hoc vien duoc dua ve buoc dang lam.
- Ban nhap chua duoc xem la onboarding hoan tat.
- Tai khoan o trang thai `ONBOARDING_IN_PROGRESS` trong thoi gian nay.

### 5.3. Thu thap nen tang hoc tap

He thong can hoi cac thong tin:

- Muc do lap trinh: chua biet, co ban, da tung hoc, co the lam du an nho.
- Cong nghe da tung hoc: C, Java, Python, HTML/CSS, JavaScript, SQL, Git, Flutter...
- Kinh nghiem hoc truoc day: tu hoc, hoc o truong, da lam project, da thuc tap.
- Diem dau can ho tro: mat goc, khong biet bat dau tu dau, yeu thuat toan, yeu frontend/backend...

Thong tin nay dung cho goi y va ho tro, khong dung de han che nguoi hoc mot cach cuc doan.

### 5.4. Thu thap muc tieu hoc

Muc tieu hoc nen cho phep chon mot muc tieu chinh va cac muc tieu phu:

- Hoc de qua mon.
- Hoc de lam do an/du an.
- Hoc de thuc tap.
- Hoc de chuyen nganh.
- Hoc de tim viec.
- Hoc de cung co nen tang lap trinh.
- Hoc mot cong nghe cu the.

Neu hoc vien chon muc tieu khong ro, he thong nen goi y lo trinh nhap mon truoc.

### 5.5. Goi y lo trinh

He thong hien thi mot hoac nhieu lo trinh phu hop voi thong tin onboarding.

Moi goi y can co:

- Ten lo trinh.
- Ly do duoc goi y.
- Doi tuong phu hop.
- Kien thuc dau vao.
- Thoi luong du kien.
- Ky nang dau ra.
- Muc do kho.

Goi y khong duoc tu dong kich hoat. Hoc vien phai tu chon va xac nhan.

### 5.6. Xac nhan onboarding

Truoc khi hoan tat, he thong hien thi man xac nhan:

- Thong tin co ban da nhap.
- Muc do hien tai.
- Muc tieu hoc.
- Lo trinh da chon.
- Quy tac mot lo trinh dang hoat dong.
- Dieu kien de doi/reset lo trinh la ngoai le can Admin xu ly.

Hoc vien phai xac nhan thong tin la dung va dong y quy tac hoc.

## 6. Luong nghiep vu chinh

```text
1. Hoc vien dang nhap sau khi xac thuc tai khoan.
2. He thong phat hien chua hoan tat onboarding.
3. He thong dua hoc vien vao buoc chao mung.
4. Hoc vien nhap thong tin co ban, nen tang, muc tieu va thoi gian hoc.
5. He thong luu nhap sau tung buoc hop le.
6. He thong hien thi danh sach lo trinh goi y.
7. Hoc vien chon mot lo trinh.
8. He thong hien thi man xac nhan.
9. Hoc vien xac nhan.
10. He thong cap nhat trang thai san sang hoc va dieu huong toi kich hoat lo trinh/dashboard.
```

## 7. Quy tac nghiep vu

| Ma | Quy tac |
|---|---|
| ONB-01 | Onboarding la bat buoc truoc khi kich hoat lo trinh va hoc noi dung rieng tu. |
| ONB-02 | Hoc vien phai xac thuc kenh lien he truoc khi onboarding duoc cong nhan hoan tat. |
| ONB-03 | Hoc vien chi chon mot lo trinh de bat dau trong onboarding. |
| ONB-04 | Goi y lo trinh chi co tinh ho tro quyet dinh, khong tu dong ep buoc. |
| ONB-05 | He thong phai cho luu nhap va tiep tuc onboarding. |
| ONB-06 | Khong thu thap giay to ca nhan/KYC trong V1. |
| ONB-07 | Sau khi xac nhan, thong tin onboarding tro thanh co so cho dashboard va ho tro hoc tap. |
| ONB-08 | Neu hoc vien muon doi lo trinh sau khi kich hoat, phai di qua quy trinh ho tro ngoai le. |

---

## 8. Trang thai va ngoai le

| Tinh huong | Cach xu ly |
|---|---|
| Hoc vien thoat giua chung | Luu nhap va tiep tuc o buoc gan nhat. |
| Thong tin bat buoc bi thieu | Khong cho qua buoc, hien thi loi ro rang. |
| Khong co lo trinh goi y phu hop | Hien thi lo trinh nhap mon hoac danh sach lo trinh pho bien. |
| Lo trinh da chon bi go khoi xuat ban truoc khi xac nhan | Yeu cau hoc vien chon lai lo trinh khac. |
| Tai khoan bi tam ngung trong onboarding | Chan hoan tat va hien thi thong bao lien he ho tro. |

---

## 9. Tieu chi hoan tat module

- Hoc vien da xac thuc co the hoan tat onboarding theo cac buoc ngan.
- He thong luu duoc ban nhap va khoi phuc buoc dang lam.
- Hoc vien nhan duoc goi y lo trinh co ly do ro rang.
- Hoc vien phai xac nhan lo trinh va quy tac hoc truoc khi ket thuc.
- Hoc vien chua onboarding khong the kich hoat lo trinh hoac hoc noi dung rieng tu.
