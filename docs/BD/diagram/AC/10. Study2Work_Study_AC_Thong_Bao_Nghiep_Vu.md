## Bieu do

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    Start([Start])
    Event["Phat sinh su kien tai khoan/onboarding/hoc tap/bai tap/noi dung/ho tro/cong dong"]
    Dedup{"Da co thong bao trung su kien?"}
    Skip["Khong tao trung lap"]
    DetermineChannel["Xac dinh kenh: in-app bat buoc, email neu quan trong"]
    CheckSetting{"Thong bao co the tat?"}
    RespectSetting["Ap dung cai dat cua hoc vien"]
    ForceMandatory["Bat buoc gui: tai khoan, bao mat, bai tap bat buoc, ho tro ngoai le"]
    CreateNotification["Tao notification voi title, body, type, actionUrl, priority"]
    Delivery["Gui in-app/email sau commit"]
    Center["Hoc vien mo trung tam thong bao"]
    ReadAction["Loc, xem chi tiet, danh dau da doc, dieu huong hanh dong"]
    AdminSend["Admin chon nhom nguoi nhan va gui thong bao lien quan"]
    End([End])

    Start --> Event --> Dedup
    Dedup -->|Co| Skip --> End
    Dedup -->|Khong| DetermineChannel --> CheckSetting
    CheckSetting -->|Khong bat buoc| RespectSetting --> CreateNotification
    CheckSetting -->|Bat buoc| ForceMandatory --> CreateNotification
    CreateNotification --> Delivery --> Center --> ReadAction --> End
    AdminSend --> DetermineChannel
```
