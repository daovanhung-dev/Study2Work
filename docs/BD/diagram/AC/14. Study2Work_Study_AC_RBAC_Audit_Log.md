# AC - Vai Tro, Phan Quyen va Audit Log

## Bieu do

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    Start([Start])
    Request["Nguoi dung/Admin thuc hien hanh dong"]
    Identify["Xac dinh user, vai tro, session, target resource"]
    Permission{"Vai tro co quyen chuc nang?"}
    Deny["Tu choi va tra loi permission state"]
    Ownership{"Dung pham vi/ownership?"}
    BusinessState{"Trang thai nghiep vu hop le?"}
    Risky{"Thao tac rui ro cao?"}
    RequireReason["Yeu cau ly do/ghi chu va canh bao tac dong"]
    Execute["Thuc hien hanh dong"]
    NeedAudit{"Can audit?"}
    WriteAudit["Ghi actor, role, action, target, before/after, reason, channel"]
    ViewAudit["Admin loc/xem audit theo hoc vien/noi dung/actor/thoi gian"]
    End([End])

    Start --> Request --> Identify --> Permission
    Permission -->|Khong| Deny --> End
    Permission -->|Co| Ownership
    Ownership -->|Khong| Deny
    Ownership -->|Co| BusinessState
    BusinessState -->|Khong| Deny
    BusinessState -->|Co| Risky
    Risky -->|Co| RequireReason --> Execute
    Risky -->|Khong| Execute
    Execute --> NeedAudit
    NeedAudit -->|Co| WriteAudit --> End
    NeedAudit -->|Khong| End
    WriteAudit --> ViewAudit
```

## Chuc nang bao phu

- Quyen Public, Learner, Content Admin, Learner Support, Community Moderator, Admin/Super Admin.
- Ma tran quyen tong quat.
- Thao tac can ghi audit.
- Xem audit log.

## Quy tac

- Quyen gan theo vai tro va muc dich nghiep vu, khong cap rong.
- Learner khong sua tien do/diem/trang thai lo trinh.
- Moderator khong sua noi dung hoc hoac tien do.
- Cap/thu hoi vai tro Admin/Support/Moderator va thao tac rui ro cao phai audit.
