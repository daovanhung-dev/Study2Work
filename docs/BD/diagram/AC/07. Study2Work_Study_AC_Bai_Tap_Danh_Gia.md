# AC - Bai Tap va Danh Gia

## Bieu do

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    Start([Start])
    OpenList["Xem danh sach bai tap tu bai hoc/chuong/khoa/dashboard"]
    PickExercise["Mo chi tiet bai tap"]
    CheckAccess{"Bai tap da mo khoa?"}
    Locked["An noi dung rieng tu va neu ly do bi khoa"]
    ShowPrompt["Hien thi muc tieu, de bai, tieu chi, goi y, tai lieu, quy dinh nop"]
    Work["Hoc vien lam bai"]
    Draft{"Can luu nhap?"}
    SaveDraft["Luu DRAFT, chua tinh hoan thanh"]
    Submit["Nop bai: quiz/text/file/link/xac nhan thuc hanh"]
    Validate{"Du truong bat buoc?"}
    Error["Hien thi loi ro rang"]
    ReviewType{"Loai danh gia?"}
    AutoGrade["Cham tu dong trac nghiem"]
    ManualReview["Chuyen UNDER_REVIEW cho Admin/Mentor"]
    Result{"Ket qua?"}
    Passed["PASSED, dong gop vao tien do neu bat buoc"]
    NeedRevision["NEEDS_REVISION, hien thi feedback"]
    Failed["FAILED, hien thi feedback"]
    CanResubmit{"Duoc nop lai?"}
    Resubmit["Hoc vien sua va nop lai"]
    End([End])

    Start --> OpenList --> PickExercise --> CheckAccess
    CheckAccess -->|Khong| Locked --> End
    CheckAccess -->|Co| ShowPrompt --> Work --> Draft
    Draft -->|Co| SaveDraft --> Work
    Draft -->|Khong| Submit
    Submit --> Validate
    Validate -->|Khong| Error --> Work
    Validate -->|Co| ReviewType
    ReviewType -->|Trac nghiem| AutoGrade --> Result
    ReviewType -->|Thu cong| ManualReview --> Result
    Result -->|Dat| Passed --> End
    Result -->|Can sua| NeedRevision --> CanResubmit
    Result -->|Khong dat| Failed --> CanResubmit
    CanResubmit -->|Co| Resubmit --> Submit
    CanResubmit -->|Khong| End
```

## Chuc nang bao phu

- Xem danh sach bai tap.
- Xem de bai.
- Luu nhap bai lam.
- Nop bai.
- Cham bai va phan hoi.
- Nop lai bai.

## Quy tac

- Ban nhap khong duoc tinh la da nop.
- Bai tap bat buoc chi dat khi submission `PASSED`.
- Bai code cham tu dong quy mo lon nam ngoai V1.
- Feedback phai ro de hoc vien biet can sua gi.
