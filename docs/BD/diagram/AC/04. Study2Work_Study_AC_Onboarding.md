# AC - Onboarding Hoc Vien

## Bieu do

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    Start([Start])
    Precheck{"Da xac thuc va khong SUSPENDED?"}
    Block["Chan onboarding, yeu cau xac thuc hoac lien he ho tro"]
    Welcome["Buoc 1: chao mung va quy tac mot lo trinh ACTIVE"]
    Basic["Buoc 2: thong tin co ban"]
    Background["Buoc 3: nen tang lap trinh va cong nghe da biet"]
    Goal["Buoc 4: muc tieu hoc chinh/phu"]
    Time["Buoc 5: thoi gian hoc du kien"]
    SaveDraft["Luu nhap sau tung buoc hop le"]
    Suggest["Goi y lo trinh theo ho so"]
    HasSuggest{"Co goi y phu hop?"}
    DefaultPath["Hien thi lo trinh nhap mon / pho bien"]
    PickPath["Hoc vien chon mot lo trinh"]
    Confirm["Xac nhan thong tin va dong y quy tac"]
    PathStillValid{"Lo trinh con duoc xuat ban?"}
    Ready["Chuyen READY_TO_LEARN hoac tiep tuc kich hoat"]
    End([End])

    Start --> Precheck
    Precheck -->|Khong| Block --> End
    Precheck -->|Co| Welcome --> Basic --> Background --> Goal --> Time
    Basic --> SaveDraft
    Background --> SaveDraft
    Goal --> SaveDraft
    Time --> SaveDraft
    SaveDraft --> Suggest
    Suggest --> HasSuggest
    HasSuggest -->|Khong| DefaultPath --> PickPath
    HasSuggest -->|Co| PickPath
    PickPath --> Confirm --> PathStillValid
    PathStillValid -->|Khong| Suggest
    PathStillValid -->|Co| Ready --> End
```

## Chuc nang bao phu

- Hien thi tien trinh onboarding.
- Luu nhap onboarding.
- Thu thap nen tang hoc tap.
- Thu thap muc tieu hoc.
- Goi y lo trinh.
- Xac nhan onboarding.

## Quy tac

- Onboarding bat buoc truoc khi kich hoat lo trinh va hoc noi dung rieng tu.
- Goi y lo trinh chi ho tro quyet dinh, khong tu dong ep buoc.
- Sau khi xac nhan, thong tin onboarding la co so cho dashboard va ho tro hoc tap.
