# AC - Tien Do Hoc Tap va Hoan Thanh

## Bieu do

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    Start([Start])
    Dashboard["Dashboard hien thi lo trinh, tien do, bai tiep theo, bai tap can xu ly"]
    Continue["Xac dinh tiep tuc hoc: gan nhat / bat buoc chua hoan thanh dau tien"]
    LearnEvent["Hoc vien xem video/doc tai lieu/lam bai tap"]
    LessonCheck{"Bai hoc dat dieu kien?"}
    LessonProgress["Cap nhat LessonProgress"]
    ChapterCheck{"Tat ca bai hoc/bai tap bat buoc cua chuong da dat?"}
    ChapterDone["Chuong COMPLETED, mo chuong tiep theo neu tuan tu"]
    CourseCheck{"Tat ca chuong/bai danh gia bat buoc cua khoa hoc da dat?"}
    CourseDone["Khoa hoc COMPLETED, hien tong ket, mo khoa hoc tiep theo"]
    PathCheck{"Tat ca khoa hoc/bai tong ket bat buoc cua lo trinh da dat?"}
    PathDone["Lo trinh COMPLETED, mo quyen chon lo trinh moi"]
    History["Ghi lich su hoc tap va thanh tich tong quan"]
    Notify["Gui thong bao hoc tap phu hop"]
    End([End])

    Start --> Dashboard --> Continue --> LearnEvent --> LessonCheck
    LessonCheck -->|Chua| Dashboard
    LessonCheck -->|Roi| LessonProgress --> ChapterCheck
    ChapterCheck -->|Chua| Dashboard
    ChapterCheck -->|Roi| ChapterDone --> CourseCheck
    CourseCheck -->|Chua| Dashboard
    CourseCheck -->|Roi| CourseDone --> PathCheck
    PathCheck -->|Chua| Dashboard
    PathCheck -->|Roi| PathDone --> History --> Notify --> End
```

## Chuc nang bao phu

- Dashboard hoc vien.
- Tiep tuc hoc.
- Tien do bai hoc.
- Tien do chuong.
- Tien do khoa hoc.
- Tien do lo trinh.
- Lich su hoc tap.

## Quy tac

- Tien do chi tinh noi dung bat buoc.
- Hoan thanh cap duoi phai cap nhat cac cap tien do lien quan.
- Hoc vien khong duoc sua truc tiep tien do.
- Khoa hoc co the tai su dung tien do neu cung phien ban xuat hien o nhieu lo trinh.
