# AC - Lo Trinh Hoc va Dang Ky Lo Trinh

## Bieu do

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    Start([Start])
    OpenDetail["Hoc vien xem chi tiet lo trinh sau dang nhap"]
    ShowState["Hien thi tien do, khoa hoc da mo/khoa/hoan thanh, nhom Zalo neu co"]
    ActivateIntent{"Chon kich hoat?"}
    CheckAccount{"Da xac thuc va onboarding?"}
    Missing["Dieu huong ve buoc con thieu"]
    CheckActive{"Da co lo trinh ACTIVE khac?"}
    BlockActive["Chan kich hoat, giai thich quy tac hoan thanh truoc"]
    Confirm["Hien thi man xac nhan: khoa hoc, thoi luong, dieu kien, quy tac"]
    CreateEnrollment["Tao LearningPathEnrollment ACTIVE"]
    Unlock["Mo khoa khoa hoc dau tien theo cau hinh"]
    MyPath["Lo trinh cua toi: tien do, danh sach khoa hoc, tiep tuc hoc"]
    CompleteCheck{"Tat ca khoa hoc/bai tap bat buoc da dat?"}
    Continue["Goi y noi dung tiep theo"]
    Complete["Chuyen COMPLETED, ghi thoi diem, mo quyen lo trinh tiep theo"]
    ReviewMode["Hoc lai/on tap lo trinh da hoan thanh"]
    SupportRequest["Gui yeu cau doi/reset/huy lo trinh"]
    End([End])

    Start --> OpenDetail --> ShowState --> ActivateIntent
    ActivateIntent -->|Khong| MyPath
    ActivateIntent -->|Co| CheckAccount
    CheckAccount -->|Khong| Missing --> End
    CheckAccount -->|Co| CheckActive
    CheckActive -->|Co| BlockActive --> SupportRequest --> End
    CheckActive -->|Khong| Confirm --> CreateEnrollment --> Unlock --> MyPath
    MyPath --> CompleteCheck
    CompleteCheck -->|Chua| Continue --> MyPath
    CompleteCheck -->|Roi| Complete --> ReviewMode --> End
```

## Chuc nang bao phu

- Xem chi tiet lo trinh sau dang nhap.
- Kich hoat lo trinh.
- Lo trinh cua toi.
- Mo khoa khoa hoc trong lo trinh.
- Hoan thanh lo trinh.
- Hoc lai lo trinh da hoan thanh.
- Yeu cau doi/reset lo trinh.

## Quy tac

- Hoc vien chi kich hoat lo trinh da xuat ban.
- Moi hoc vien chi co toi da mot lo trinh `ACTIVE`.
- Doi/reset/huy lo trinh la ngoai le do Admin xu ly va ghi ly do.
- Zalo khong phai dieu kien hoan thanh.
