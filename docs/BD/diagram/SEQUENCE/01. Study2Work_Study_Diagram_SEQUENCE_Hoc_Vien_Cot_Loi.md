# Study2Work Study - SEQUENCE Diagram Hoc Vien Cot Loi

## Nguon phan tich

- Toan bo 14 tai lieu trong `S2W/docs/BD`.
- Trong tam: dang ky/xac thuc, onboarding, kich hoat lo trinh, hoc bai, nop bai, cap nhat tien do, hoan thanh, thong bao, Zalo va ho tro ngoai le.
- Day la sequence tong quan. Sequence chi tiet kem request/response JSON nam trong cac file `02` den `14` cung thu muc.

## Bieu do

```mermaid
sequenceDiagram
    autonumber
    participant U as Guest / Learner
    participant Catalog as Public Catalog
    participant Auth as Account Service
    participant Onboarding as Onboarding Service
    participant Path as Learning Path Service
    participant Content as Course Content Service
    participant Exercise as Exercise Service
    participant Progress as Progress Service
    participant Notify as Notification Service
    participant Community as Community Service
    participant Support as Learner Support
    participant Audit as Audit Log

    U->>Catalog: Xem danh sach va chi tiet lo trinh / khoa hoc
    Catalog-->>U: Hien thi noi dung cong khai va bai hoc mau neu co

    U->>Auth: Dang ky hoac dang nhap
    Auth-->>U: Tao phien va trang thai tai khoan

    alt Tai khoan chua xac thuc
        Auth-->>U: Yeu cau xac thuc email / phone
        U->>Auth: Gui ma OTP hoac mo link xac thuc
        Auth-->>U: Chuyen trang thai VERIFIED
        Notify-->>U: Thong bao xac thuc thanh cong
    else Tai khoan bi SUSPENDED
        Auth-->>U: Tu choi hanh dong hoc tap
    end

    alt Chua hoan tat onboarding
        U->>Onboarding: Nhap ho so, nen tang, muc tieu va thoi gian hoc
        Onboarding-->>U: Luu nhap sau tung buoc hop le
        Onboarding->>Path: Lay danh sach lo trinh goi y
        Path-->>Onboarding: Tra ve lo trinh phu hop
        Onboarding-->>U: Hien thi goi y va ly do
        U->>Onboarding: Chon mot lo trinh va xac nhan quy tac
        Onboarding->>Path: Ghi lua chon lo trinh
        Onboarding-->>U: Chuyen trang thai READY_TO_LEARN
    end

    U->>Path: Yeu cau kich hoat lo trinh da chon
    Path->>Auth: Kiem tra xac thuc, onboarding, trang thai tai khoan
    Auth-->>Path: Hop le
    Path->>Path: Kiem tra khong co lo trinh ACTIVE khac

    alt Du dieu kien kich hoat
        Path->>Progress: Tao enrollment va tien do ban dau
        Progress-->>Path: Tien do 0 phan tram, mo noi dung dau tien
        Path->>Notify: Tao thong bao lo trinh da kich hoat
        Path-->>U: Dieu huong dashboard
    else Dang co lo trinh ACTIVE khac
        Path-->>U: Tu choi kich hoat lo trinh moi
        U->>Support: Gui yeu cau doi / reset / huy lo trinh
        Support->>Progress: Xem tien do va lich su hoc
        Support->>Path: Xem lo trinh hien tai va lo trinh dich
        Support->>Audit: Ghi quyet dinh va ly do
        alt Admin chap thuan
            Support->>Path: Reset / huy / chuyen lo trinh
            Path->>Progress: Cap nhat trang thai lien quan
            Support->>Notify: Thong bao ket qua cho hoc vien
        else Admin tu choi
            Support->>Notify: Thong bao tu choi va ly do
        end
    end

    loop Hoc tung noi dung bat buoc
        U->>Content: Mo khoa hoc / chuong / bai hoc
        Content->>Path: Kiem tra quyen truy cap va mo khoa
        Path-->>Content: Cho phep neu dung lo trinh va trang thai
        Content-->>U: Hien thi video, tai lieu, bai tap lien quan
        U->>Content: Xem video / doc tai lieu / danh dau hoan thanh
        Content->>Progress: Ghi dieu kien bai hoc da dat

        opt Bai hoc co bai tap bat buoc
            U->>Exercise: Luu nhap va nop bai
            Exercise->>Exercise: Cham trac nghiem hoac chuyen review thu cong
            Exercise-->>U: Tra trang thai PASSED / NEEDS_REVISION / FAILED
            alt Can sua
                Notify-->>U: Thong bao can sua va feedback
                U->>Exercise: Nop lai neu duoc phep
            else Dat yeu cau
                Exercise->>Progress: Ghi bai tap bat buoc da dat
            end
        end

        Progress->>Progress: Cap nhat bai hoc, chuong, khoa hoc, lo trinh
        Progress-->>U: Goi y noi dung tiep theo
    end

    alt Lo trinh hoan thanh
        Progress->>Path: Bao tat ca dieu kien bat buoc da dat
        Path->>Progress: Chuyen enrollment sang COMPLETED
        Path->>Notify: Tao thong bao chuc mung va goi y tiep theo
        Notify-->>U: Hien thi tong ket va quyen chon lo trinh moi
    end

    opt Hoc vien muon vao nhom Zalo
        U->>Community: Xem nhom phu hop
        Community->>Path: Kiem tra quyen theo lo trinh / khoa hoc
        Community-->>U: Hien thi quy tac cong dong
        U->>Community: Xac nhan da doc quy tac
        Community->>Community: Ghi su kien mo link
        Community-->>U: Mo link Zalo ben ngoai
    end
```

## Giai thich chi tiet

### Pha kham pha va tai khoan

- `Public Catalog` chi tra ve noi dung cong khai: danh sach/chi tiet lo trinh, khoa hoc va bai hoc mau.
- `Account Service` quan ly dang ky, dang nhap, xac thuc kenh lien he va trang thai `SUSPENDED`.
- Tai khoan chua xac thuc khong duoc kich hoat lo trinh, hoc noi dung rieng tu, luu tien do chinh thuc hoac nop bai.

### Pha onboarding

- `Onboarding Service` luu nhap theo tung buoc, thu thap nen tang, muc tieu va thoi gian hoc.
- Goi y lo trinh den tu `Learning Path Service`, nhung hoc vien phai tu chon va xac nhan.
- Sau khi xac nhan, hoc vien dat trang thai `READY_TO_LEARN`.

### Pha kich hoat lo trinh

- `Learning Path Service` kiem tra xac thuc, onboarding, tai khoan khong bi tam ngung va khong co lo trinh `ACTIVE` khac.
- Neu hop le, he thong tao enrollment, tien do 0 phan tram, mo noi dung dau tien va gui thong bao.
- Neu da co lo trinh active, yeu cau moi bi tu choi. Doi/reset/huy chi di qua `Learner Support` va phai ghi `Audit Log`.

### Pha hoc va danh gia

- `Course Content Service` chi hien thi noi dung rieng tu neu quyen truy cap va dieu kien mo khoa hop le.
- `Progress Service` ghi nhan dieu kien bai hoc va cap nhat tien do tu bai hoc len chuong, khoa hoc, lo trinh.
- `Exercise Service` xu ly luu nhap, nop bai, cham trac nghiem, review thu cong va nop lai. Chi ket qua dat moi tinh cho bai tap bat buoc.

### Pha hoan thanh va cong dong

- Khi tat ca noi dung bat buoc dat yeu cau, lo trinh chuyen `COMPLETED`, he thong gui thong bao tong ket va mo quyen chon lo trinh moi.
- `Community Service` chi mo link Zalo sau khi hoc vien du quyen va xac nhan quy tac. Study khong doc thanh vien, tin nhan hay noi dung noi bo cua Zalo trong V1.
