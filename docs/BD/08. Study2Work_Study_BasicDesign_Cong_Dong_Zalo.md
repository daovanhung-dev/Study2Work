# Study2Work - Study
## Basic Design: Cong Dong Zalo

> **STT:** 08  
> **Nguon:** `0. Study2Work_Study_Business_Description.md`  
> **Pham vi:** Mo ta chuc nang hien thi va quan ly lien ket nhom Zalo o muc nghiep vu. Khong mo ta tich hop API Zalo, dong bo thanh vien/tin nhan hoac xu ly du lieu ben ngoai.

---

## 1. Muc tieu module

Module Cong dong Zalo tao khong gian ho tro ngoai he thong de hoc vien hoi dap, chia se tai lieu va duy tri dong luc hoc.

Muc tieu:

- Hien thi nhom Zalo phu hop voi lo trinh, khoa hoc hoac chu de.
- Yeu cau hoc vien doc va xac nhan quy tac cong dong truoc khi mo lien ket.
- Ghi nhan su kien mo link de do nhu cau cong dong.
- Quan ly danh sach nhom, mo ta, quy tac va moderator.
- Dam bao viec tham gia Zalo la tu nguyen va khong anh huong dieu kien hoan thanh V1.

---

## 2. Doi tuong su dung

| Vai tro | Chuc nang lien quan |
|---|---|
| Learner | Xem nhom phu hop, doc quy tac, mo link Zalo. |
| Guest | Xem gioi thieu cong dong cong khai neu Admin cho phep, khong xem link rieng tu. |
| Community Moderator | Quan ly thong tin nhom, quy tac va tiep nhan bao cao. |
| Admin | Tao/cap nhat/an/hien nhom cong dong. |

---

## 3. Pham vi nhom Zalo

Mot nhom Zalo co the gan voi:

- Lo trinh hoc.
- Khoa hoc.
- Chu de hoc tap.
- Nhom ho tro chung cho nguoi moi.

Moi nhom can co:

- Ten nhom.
- Mo ta muc tieu nhom.
- Pham vi ap dung.
- Lien ket tham gia.
- Quy tac nhom.
- Moderator/phu trach.
- Trang thai hoat dong.
- Dieu kien hien thi.

---

## 4. Chuc nang chi tiet

### 4.1. Hien thi nhom phu hop

Hoc vien co the thay nhom Zalo tai:

- Chi tiet lo trinh.
- Lo trinh cua toi.
- Chi tiet khoa hoc.
- Man hoc bai neu nhom lien quan toi khoa/chu de.
- Dashboard hoc vien.

Thong tin hien thi:

- Ten nhom.
- Mo ta ngan.
- Pham vi nhom.
- Moderator/phu trach neu co.
- Trang thai: dang hoat dong, tam dung, day thanh vien, sap thay link.
- Nut mo link neu hoc vien du quyen.

### 4.2. Dieu kien hien thi link Zalo

Quy tac:

- Guest khong xem link nhom chi danh cho hoc vien.
- Hoc vien chi xem link nhom gan voi lo trinh/khoa hoc da duoc cap quyen.
- Nhom cong khai co the hien thi gioi thieu nhung van co the an link neu Admin cau hinh.
- Nhom da tam dung hoac luu tru khong hien thi nut tham gia.

### 4.3. Xac nhan quy tac truoc khi mo link

Khi hoc vien bam `Tham gia nhom Zalo`, he thong can:

```text
1. Hien thi quy tac cong dong.
2. Yeu cau hoc vien xac nhan da doc va dong y.
3. Mo lien ket Zalo ben ngoai.
4. Ghi nhan su kien da mo lien ket neu can do luong.
```

He thong chi ghi nhan "da mo lien ket", khong khang dinh hoc vien da that su tham gia nhom.

### 4.4. Quy tac cong dong toi thieu

Quy tac can hien thi ro:

- Ton trong thanh vien khac.
- Khong spam, quang cao, lua dao hoac phat tan link doc hai.
- Khong chia se dap an bai tap theo huong lam ho.
- Uu tien goi y, giai thich tu duy va dan tai lieu.
- Khong chia se du lieu ca nhan cua nguoi khac.
- Moderator co quyen xu ly thanh vien theo quy dinh nhom va quy dinh noi bo.

### 4.5. Bao cao van de cong dong

Hoc vien can co cach bao cao:

- Link Zalo hong.
- Nhom khong dung noi dung.
- Nhom co spam/lua dao.
- Moderator khong phu hop.
- Quy tac nhom khong ro.

Bao cao duoc chuyen cho Moderator/Admin xu ly.

### 4.6. Quan ly nhom cong dong

Admin/Moderator co the:

- Tao nhom cong dong moi.
- Gan nhom voi lo trinh, khoa hoc hoac chu de.
- Cap nhat link tham gia.
- Cap nhat mo ta va quy tac nhom.
- Doi trang thai hoat dong.
- Gan nguoi phu trach/moderator.
- An nhom neu link hong, nhom day hoac khong con phu hop.

---

## 5. Luong nghiep vu chinh

### 5.1. Hoc vien mo link Zalo

```text
1. Hoc vien mo dashboard/lo trinh/khoa hoc.
2. He thong hien thi nhom Zalo phu hop theo quyen.
3. Hoc vien chon Tham gia nhom Zalo.
4. He thong hien thi quy tac cong dong.
5. Hoc vien xac nhan da doc.
6. He thong mo link Zalo ben ngoai.
7. He thong ghi nhan su kien mo link neu duoc cau hinh.
```

### 5.2. Admin tam dung nhom

```text
1. Admin phat hien link hong hoac nhom khong con phu hop.
2. Admin doi trang thai nhom sang tam dung/luu tru.
3. He thong an nut tham gia nhom.
4. He thong co the thong bao cho hoc vien dang hoc neu nhom lien quan quan trong.
```

## 7. Tieu chi hoan tat module

- Hoc vien thay duoc nhom Zalo phu hop voi lo trinh/khoa hoc.
- Guest khong xem duoc link nhom rieng tu.
- Hoc vien phai xac nhan quy tac truoc khi mo link.
- He thong ghi nhan duoc su kien mo link o muc nghiep vu neu can do luong.
- Admin/Moderator co the quan ly thong tin va trang thai nhom.
