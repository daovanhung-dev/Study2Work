# Study2Work - Study
## Basic Design: Tien Do Hoc Tap va Hoan Thanh

> **STT:** 07  
> **Nguon:** `0. Study2Work_Study_Business_Description.md`  
> **Pham vi:** Mo ta chuc nang theo doi tien do, dashboard hoc vien, dieu kien hoan thanh va tong ket ket qua. Khong mo ta cong thuc luu tru, API hoac xu ly du lieu ky thuat.

---

## 1. Muc tieu module

Module Tien do giup hoc vien biet minh dang o dau, can lam gi tiep theo va da tien bo ra sao. Dong thoi, module cung cap co so nghiep vu de he thong mo khoa noi dung va xac dinh khi nao bai hoc, chuong, khoa hoc, lo trinh duoc hoan thanh.

Muc tieu:

- Hien thi hanh dong hoc tiep theo tren dashboard.
- Theo doi tien do o cap bai hoc, chuong, khoa hoc, lo trinh va ho so hoc vien.
- Tinh hoan thanh dua tren noi dung bat buoc.
- Ho tro hoc vien tiep tuc hoc o moc gan nhat.
- Cung cap tong ket khi hoan thanh khoa hoc/lo trinh.

---

## 2. Doi tuong su dung

| Vai tro | Chuc nang lien quan |
|---|---|
| Learner | Xem dashboard, tien do, bai tiep theo, bai tap can lam va tong ket. |
| Admin | Xem chi so tien do tong hop qua module bao cao. |
| Learner Support | Xem tien do ca nhan de ho tro ngoai le. |

---

## 3. Cac cap tien do

```text
Bai hoc -> Chuong -> Khoa hoc -> Lo trinh -> Ho so hoc vien
```

| Cap | Cach tinh nghiep vu |
|---|---|
| Bai hoc | Hoan thanh khi dat dieu kien cua bai hoc. |
| Chuong | Hoan thanh khi toan bo noi dung bat buoc trong chuong hoan thanh. |
| Khoa hoc | Hoan thanh khi cac chuong bat buoc hoan thanh va dat dieu kien danh gia. |
| Lo trinh | Hoan thanh khi toan bo khoa hoc bat buoc hoan thanh. |
| Ho so hoc vien | Tong hop so lo trinh, khoa hoc, gio hoc, bai tap va thanh tich. |

---

## 4. Chuc nang chi tiet

### 4.1. Dashboard hoc vien

Dashboard can uu tien hanh dong tiep theo, khong chi hien thi so lieu.

Noi dung can co:

- Lo trinh dang hoc.
- Phan tram tien do lo trinh.
- Khoa hoc dang hoc.
- Bai hoc can tiep tuc.
- Bai tap chua hoan thanh hoac can sua.
- Chuong/khoa hoc vua hoan thanh.
- Thong bao moi.
- Nhom Zalo lien quan neu co.
- Goi y quay lai hoc neu lau ngay chua hoc.

Hanh dong chinh:

- `Tiep tuc hoc`.
- `Lam bai tap can xu ly`.
- `Xem lo trinh cua toi`.
- `Xem tien do chi tiet`.

### 4.2. Tiep tuc hoc

He thong can xac dinh noi dung tiep theo dua tren:

- Bai hoc dang hoc gan nhat.
- Bai hoc bat buoc chua hoan thanh dau tien.
- Bai tap bat buoc chua dat.
- Thu tu mo khoa cua lo trinh/khoa hoc.

Neu khong co bai hoc dang do, he thong goi y noi dung bat buoc tiep theo. Neu lo trinh da hoan thanh, he thong hien thi tong ket va goi y lo trinh tiep theo.

### 4.3. Tien do bai hoc

Bai hoc co the duoc danh dau hoan thanh khi thoa cau hinh Admin:

- Xem video dat ty le toi thieu, vi du 80%.
- Hoc vien xac nhan da hoc xong.
- Doc/xac nhan tai lieu bat buoc.
- Vuot cau hoi tu kiem tra.
- Hoan thanh bai tap lien quan.

Neu co nhieu dieu kien, he thong can hien thi dieu kien nao da dat va con thieu.

### 4.4. Tien do chuong

Chuong hoan thanh khi:

- Tat ca bai hoc bat buoc hoan thanh.
- Cac bai tap/bai kiem tra bat buoc trong chuong dat yeu cau.

Khi chuong hoan thanh:

- He thong hien thi thong bao hoan thanh chuong.
- He thong mo chuong tiep theo neu cau hinh tuan tu.
- He thong cap nhat tien do khoa hoc.

### 4.5. Tien do khoa hoc

Khoa hoc hoan thanh khi:

- Tat ca chuong bat buoc hoan thanh.
- Diem tong hop dat nguong neu co cau hinh.
- Project/lab cuoi khoa da dat neu khoa hoc yeu cau.

Khi khoa hoc hoan thanh:

- He thong ghi nhan thoi diem hoan thanh.
- He thong hien thi tong ket khoa hoc.
- He thong mo khoa khoa hoc tiep theo trong lo trinh neu du dieu kien.

### 4.6. Tien do lo trinh

Lo trinh hoan thanh khi:

- Tat ca khoa hoc bat buoc hoan thanh.
- Cac bai tap tong ket bat buoc dat yeu cau.
- Khong con noi dung bat buoc o trang thai chua hoan thanh.

Khi lo trinh hoan thanh:

- Trang thai lo trinh chuyen `COMPLETED`.
- He thong ghi thoi diem hoan thanh.
- He thong tong ket ky nang/khoa hoc da hoan thanh.
- He thong mo quyen chon lo trinh moi.
- He thong gui thong bao chuc mung va goi y huong tiep theo.

### 4.7. Lich su hoc tap

Hoc vien can xem duoc lich su o muc tong quan:

- Lo trinh da hoan thanh.
- Khoa hoc da hoan thanh.
- Bai tap da nop.
- Ket qua danh gia quan trong.
- Noi dung dang hoc gan day.

Lich su khong cho phep hoc vien sua trang thai tien do.

## 5. Luong nghiep vu chinh

### 5.1. Cap nhat tien do sau bai hoc

```text
1. Hoc vien mo bai hoc.
2. He thong tai trang thai tien do hien tai.
3. Hoc vien xem video/doc tai lieu/lam bai tap.
4. He thong ghi nhan cac dieu kien da dat.
5. Neu bai hoc dat dieu kien, bai hoc chuyen hoan thanh.
6. He thong cap nhat chuong, khoa hoc va lo trinh lien quan.
7. He thong goi y noi dung tiep theo.
```

### 5.2. Hoan thanh khoa hoc

```text
1. He thong kiem tra cac chuong bat buoc.
2. He thong kiem tra bai tap/bai danh gia bat buoc.
3. Neu du dieu kien, khoa hoc chuyen COMPLETED.
4. He thong hien thi tong ket khoa hoc.
5. He thong mo khoa hoc tiep theo trong lo trinh.
```

## 6. Quy tac nghiep vu

| Ma | Quy tac |
|---|---|
| PRG-01 | Tien do chi tinh tu noi dung duoc Admin danh dau bat buoc. |
| PRG-02 | Noi dung tuy chon co the hien thi trong lich su nhung khong chan hoan thanh. |
| PRG-03 | Hoc vien khong duoc sua truc tiep tien do cua minh. |
| PRG-04 | He thong phai giu kha nang tiep tuc hoc sau khi doi thiet bi hoac tai lai trang. |
| PRG-05 | Hoan thanh bai hoc/chuong/khoa hoc phai cap nhat cac cap tien do lien quan. |
| PRG-06 | Neu noi dung dang hoc bi cap nhat quan trong, he thong can thong bao anh huong den tien do. |
| PRG-07 | Tien do khoa hoc co the tai su dung neu cung mot phien ban khoa hoc xuat hien o nhieu lo trinh, nhung dieu kien lo trinh van tinh rieng. |

## 7. Tieu chi hoan tat module

- Dashboard hien thi dung lo trinh, tien do va hanh dong tiep theo.
- Hoc vien co the xem tien do chi tiet theo bai hoc, chuong, khoa hoc va lo trinh.
- He thong chi tinh noi dung bat buoc vao tien do hoan thanh.
- Noi dung tiep theo duoc goi y dung theo quy tac mo khoa.
- Khi khoa hoc/lo trinh hoan thanh, hoc vien thay tong ket va quyen tiep theo.
